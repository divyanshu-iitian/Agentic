---
name: research
description: Gather and compare information while keeping facts traceable.
triggers:
  - research
  - compare
  - investigate
  - latest information
---

1. Restate the decision or question being researched.
2. Prefer primary sources and note the date of time-sensitive facts.
3. Separate source-backed facts from inference.
4. Compare options using the criteria that matter to the user.
5. Keep links or source names alongside the claim they support.
6. End with a concise recommendation and the main tradeoff.
